Privacy Policy

How We Protect Your Information

Effective date: May 2025

"We collect only what we need, use it only to serve you, and never sell your data to anyone."

📋 Information We Collect

When you place an order with Lunère™ Boutique, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full name
  • WhatsApp phone number
  • City and full delivery address
  • Order details (products, sizes, colours)
  • Payment method selected (bank transfer or cash USD)
  • Payment receipt / screenshot (if provided)

🎯 How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your order
  • To contact you via WhatsApp about your order status
  • To arrange delivery to your address
  • To send you order confirmation and tracking updates

🔒 Data Storage & Security

Your order data is stored securely within our order management system. We do not store your bank account details or payment credentials. Payment receipts are used only for verification purposes and are not shared.

🚫 What We Never Do

  • We never sell your personal data to third parties
  • We never share your information with advertisers
  • We never use your data for purposes other than fulfilling your order
  • We never send unsolicited marketing messages without your consent

📱 WhatsApp Communication

By placing an order, you consent to receiving order-related messages via WhatsApp on the number you provide. We will only send you messages related to your order — confirmation, updates, and delivery notifications.

✉️ Your Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your personal data after your order is completed. Contact us at Lunereboutique.ly@gmail.com and we will process your request within 7 business days.

Returns & Exchanges

Return & Exchange Policy

Effective date: May 2025

"Due to the nature of international sourcing, all sales are final once the order is confirmed and payment is received."

✅ When We Accept Returns

We accept returns or exchanges only in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • The item received is significantly different from what was ordered (wrong product)
  • The item arrived with a manufacturing defect that was not disclosed
  • The item arrived visibly damaged due to shipping

❌ Non-Returnable Situations

  • Change of mind after order confirmation
  • Incorrect size selected by the customer
  • Minor colour variation from screen display
  • Pre-order items under any circumstances
  • Items that have been used, worn, or washed
  • Items without original packaging

📝 How to Request a Return

  • Contact us within 48 hours of receiving your order
  • Send photos of the item clearly showing the issue via WhatsApp
  • Include your order number in the message
  • Our team will review and respond within 2 business days
📸

Do not return any item before receiving written approval from our team. Returns without prior approval will not be accepted.

💰 Refunds

If a return is approved, refunds will be processed within 7–14 business days via the same payment method used for the original order. Shipping costs are non-refundable.
